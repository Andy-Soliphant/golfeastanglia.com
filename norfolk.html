Norfolk Golf Courses

Where Links Golf
Meets Open Sky

From the windswept magnificence of Royal West Norfolk to heathland gems and stay & play retreats — Norfolk's golf courses reward those who seek them out. We know every one personally.

Signature
Signature Courses

World-ranked. Historically significant. The courses that define a Golf East Anglia journey — each a destination in its own right, best experienced as part of a curated Golf East Anglia itinerary.

Links · Brancaster · North Norfolk

Royal West Norfolk

Ranked 42nd in Britain & Ireland and among the world's great links. Reached across a tidal causeway — plan around the tides. Strictly limited visitor access. Playing here feels like stepping into the very heart of golf's history.

Links · Hunstanton · North West Norfolk

Hunstanton Golf Club

Consistently in England's Top 20 and a regular host of national qualifying. Set along the Wash with natural duneland fairways and exceptional par threes. A technically demanding course that rewards creative, ground-level golf.

Top 20 EnglandLinksChampionshipQualifying Venue

Clifftop Links · Cromer · North Norfolk

Royal Cromer

Founded 1888. Royal patronage granted before the end of that century. Perched on the highest clifftop on England's east coast with the North Sea stretching to the horizon from almost every hole. Visually arresting, genuinely demanding.

Est. 1888ClifftopSea ViewsRoyal

Heathland · West Suffolk · The Sacred Nine

Royal Worlington & Newmarket

Bernard Darwin named it "the sacred nine" in 1928. Nine holes played twice, consistently ranked in the Top 100 of Britain & Ireland. Forty acres of west Suffolk heathland. More history per hole than almost anywhere else in the game.

Top 100 GB&INine HolesHeathlandPilgrimage

Links · Suffolk Heritage Coast

Aldeburgh Golf Club

A championship heathland links on the Suffolk Heritage Coast — home of the Aldeburgh Festival, one of England's finest musical events. The course runs through gorse and heather with the sea glimpsed between the dunes. Understated, challenging, quintessentially English.

ChampionshipHeathland LinksSuffolk CoastHeritage

Heathland · Thorpeness · Suffolk Coast

Thorpeness Golf Club

Designed by James Braid — five times Open Champion — and largely unchanged since 1923. An inland heathland course of exceptional character, carved through mature pine and heather within walking distance of the sea. One of Suffolk's best-kept secrets.

James Braid DesignHeathlandHistoricSuffolk
Classic
Classic Courses

Well-established courses of genuine quality — excellent additions to any multi-day itinerary. These courses reward the golfer who wants depth and variety alongside the Signature experiences.

Clifftop · Sheringham · North Norfolk

Sheringham Golf Club

A shorter but technically demanding clifftop course with some of the best character on the Norfolk coast. Perched between the cliff edge and the town, with spectacular upper-hole views. Pairs naturally with Royal Cromer.

ClifftopNorth NorfolkSea Views

Heathland · Woodbridge · Suffolk

Woodbridge Golf Club

A superb heathland course in the heart of Suffolk — pine, heather and tight, well-defined fairways. Consistently well-maintained and a perfect complement to the coastal courses. Popular with county golfers for good reason.

HeathlandSuffolkWell-Maintained

Parkland · Norwich

Royal Norwich

Recently redesigned to championship standard on the edge of the city. A modern parkland layout with strong bunkering and strategic variety — the strongest course in the Norwich area and a solid anchor for a city-based trip.

ParklandRedesignedRoyal

Heathland · Thetford · Breckland

Thetford Golf Club

A fine heathland course in the Breckland forest — pine, silver birch and heather fairways with a classic feel. An excellent companion course to Royal Worlington for a Breckland golf day.

HeathlandBrecklandForest

Parkland · Ipswich · Suffolk

Ipswich Golf Club (Purdis Heath)

A heathland parkland course on the edge of Ipswich with an excellent reputation among serious golfers. Well-regarded for its condition and variety — a strong option for groups based in south Suffolk.

Heathland ParklandSuffolk

Links · Felixstowe · Suffolk Coast

Felixstowe Ferry Golf Club

A genuine links at the mouth of the River Deben — one of the few true seaside links courses in Suffolk. Exposed, unpretentious, and with a character entirely its own. An authentic links experience at the southern end of the East Anglia circuit.

LinksCoastalSuffolk

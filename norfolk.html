Norfolk Golf Courses

Where Links Golf
Meets Open Sky

From the windswept magnificence of Royal West Norfolk to heathland gems and stay & play retreats — Norfolk's golf courses reward those who seek them out. We know every one personally.

Premium
Signature Courses

Our flagship selections — exceptional courses that define a Norfolk golf break. Each is a destination in its own right, best experienced as part of a curated Golf East Anglia itinerary.

Links · Brancaster

Royal West Norfolk

One of England's great links — a timeless, members-only gem perched between tidal marshes and the North Sea. Playing here feels like stepping into golf's history. Access is carefully managed and visitor numbers are strictly limited.

Links Historic Visitor Access Championship

Links · Cromer

Royal Cromer

The clifftop links at Cromer offers dramatic North Sea views at every turn. One of England's most scenic courses, it combines genuine links challenge with an atmosphere that is quintessentially Norfolk — proud, unhurried and memorable.

Clifftop Links Sea Views Historic

Heathland · Thetford

Royal Worlington & Newmarket

Described by Bernard Darwin as "the sacred nine" — the finest nine-hole course in the world. This heathland classic in the Breckland forest is a pilgrimage for any serious golfer. Explore our dedicated Worlington page.

Nine Holes Heathland Historic Unique

Parkland · Hunstanton

Hunstanton Golf Club

A superb links on the west Norfolk coast, Hunstanton sits just south of the Wash and is regularly used for regional qualifying. Combines well with Royal West Norfolk on a coastal two-course itinerary — see our Norfolk Coast itineraries.

Links West Coast Championship

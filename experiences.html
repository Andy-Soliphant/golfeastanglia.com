Experience one · Optional add-on

PGA Professional
session with Pascal

Links golf — especially on the north Norfolk coast — is a different game. The wind, the firm turf, the bump-and-run — none of it comes naturally to golfers who have spent their playing lives on parkland courses. A session with Pascal Ogden — former EuroPro Tour player, PGA professional, and the coach who took our founder's handicap from 28 to 8 in twelve months — is built specifically around the courses you are about to play. Not a generic lesson. Preparation for a specific round, on a specific course, in conditions you will actually face.

Experience two · The pilgrimage

Royal Worlington
& the Sacred Nine

The finest nine-hole course in the world. Named the Sacred Nine by Bernard Darwin in 1928, Royal Worlington & Newmarket has been consistently ranked in the Top 100 of Britain and Ireland for decades. Forty acres of west Suffolk heathland. More history per hole than almost anywhere else in the game. For the golfer who knows, this is a pilgrimage.

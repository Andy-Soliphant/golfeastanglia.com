The proposition

Two counties.
A lifetime of
golf to explore.

Norfolk and Suffolk offer the rarest combination in English golf — world-class links courses on the North Sea coast, and inland heathland classics of the very highest quality. All within easy reach of London, and yet almost entirely unmarketed to the international visitor.

Golf East Anglia exists to change that. We package the region properly: curated itineraries, handpicked accommodation, and the local knowledge to make every round count. Whether you are a solo golfer, a group, or a travel agent — this is the specialist you have been looking for. We offer bespoke golf experiences alongside every itinerary.

01
Links

North Norfolk coast

Royal West Norfolk, Hunstanton, Royal Cromer and Sheringham — four of England's finest links, all within 30 miles of each other. Including one World Top 100 course.

02
Heathland

Suffolk coastal golf

Aldeburgh, Thorpeness, Woodbridge and Felixstowe Ferry — gorse, heather and fast-draining sandy soil. Playable 12 months a year. Largely unknown to the international visitor.

03
Bespoke stays

Accommodation to match

From North Norfolk flint farmhouses to Suffolk coastal retreats. Every stay chosen to complete the journey — not just fill a bed between rounds.

The proposition

A genuine specialist product in an underserviced region.

Norfolk and Suffolk represent one of England's most significant and least exploited golf travel opportunities. Two World and GB&I Top 100 courses. Two Royal clubs. Four Suffolk courses in the NCG England Top 100. Links turf on the east coast that competes with anything in the country. And until very recently, no specialist operator packaging it properly for the international visitor.

Golf East Anglia fills that gap. We have built the product — the itineraries, the course relationships, the logistical knowledge, and the booking infrastructure — to deliver a premium East Anglia golf programme that you can confidently put in front of your clients. We handle everything from tee time to tide table. You handle the client relationship.

The opportunity

Two counties. Almost entirely unknown to the international visitor.

Royal West Norfolk at Brancaster is ranked in the world's top 100. Hunstanton is a championship links compared by serious golfers to the great west coast Scottish courses. Royal Cromer has welcomed visitors since 1888. Aldeburgh and Thorpeness are heathland classics praised by Bernard Darwin.

These courses are within 2.5 hours of London by road and 90 minutes by train from Liverpool Street. Stansted Airport is 60 miles from the Suffolk Heritage Coast. The infrastructure is straightforward. The gap in the market is real.

The product

Four itinerary structures. Built to be customised.

Our four core itinerary frameworks cover the full range of what a visiting golfer might want from East Anglia. Each is a starting point, not a fixed product. Group size, specific course preferences, budget point, accommodation level, and pace are all variables we adjust around each enquiry.

Client protection

Your clients remain your clients. Always.

We understand that the willingness to introduce a client to a specialist supplier rests entirely on trust that the supplier will not use that introduction to build a direct relationship. This is one of the oldest problems in the travel trade, and it is one we take seriously.

Our commitment is explicit and unconditional: we will never approach, market to, retain contact with, or solicit future business from any client introduced through a travel agent or operator partner — whether by email, phone, or any other channel.

Our commercial interest is in being a reliable and valued part of your supply chain for the long term. A repeat booking that comes through you is worth more to us than a direct booking that poisons the relationship that made it possible.

No direct marketing to introduced clients — ever, under any circumstances.
No retention of client contact details for any purpose beyond fulfilling the booking made through you.
No follow-up solicitation — post-trip emails, newsletters, offers, or any other outbound communication to your clients.
Repeat bookings flow through you — if a client contacts us directly for a repeat trip, we refer them back to the originating agent.
Transparent pricing — your clients will never be quoted a lower rate by going direct to us than the rate you present to them.

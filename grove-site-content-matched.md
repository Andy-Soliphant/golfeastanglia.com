## The Grove

Cromer

A Georgian country house dating back some 250 years, run as a family guest house since 1936. Set within four acres above Cromer's clifftops, with its own kitchen garden, indoor heated pool and a short woodland walk down to the beach. The Garden Rooms restaurant cooks straight from the estate's greenhouse, and Sundown — a pair of tipis on the front lawn — brings wood-fired pizzas and easy evenings under festoon lights. Five minutes from Royal Cromer.

Badges: Kitchen Garden · Indoor Pool · Georgian House · 5 mins Royal Cromer

The experience

Learn it in the morning.
Play it in the afternoon.

Pascal's coaching has been shaped by direct mentorship from a current world top-ten PGA player. Two hours of focused technical work each morning. That afternoon, 18 holes on a championship course — applying exactly what you learned. The feedback loop between lesson and real course is what makes the improvement tangible within the week.


Your coach is half-French and a native speaker — which means that when he is not on the practice ground, the group moves through France as insiders rather than visitors. His language and cultural knowledge are at your disposal throughout; the logistics, bookings and organisation are handled by the Golf East Anglia team so that his focus remains entirely on your game.

01

World-class coaching

Pascal's methodology comes directly from a current top-ten PGA player. Every morning session has one focused theme, with professional data capture and video analysis. A written development plan goes home with every guest.

02

Exceptional courses

La Mer, Les Pins and Belle Dune — three courses of genuine championship pedigree that most British golfers have never played. La Mer has hosted six French Opens. Les Pins is a Tom Simpson classic. Belle Dune is one of France's most photographed courses.

03

The wine evening

A dedicated French sommelier joins the group on the third evening for a private seated tasting of five wines — one from each great French region. Then, on the way home, a guided stop at Calais Vins to take the experience home.

04

Genuinely small

Ten guests maximum. Not a compromise — a deliberate choice. Real individual attention from your coach, dinners that feel like a private gathering, and a group small enough that everyone knows each other by the second day. The intimacy is the product.
